ShareChef Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and login credentials, stored securely with Supabase. Recipes you save and content you share are stored with your account. If you use voice features, your speech is transmitted to OpenAI to generate Micheli's responses; ShareChef does not permanently store your voice recordings.

How we use your data

Your data is used only to provide ShareChef's features: generating recipes, powering voice conversations, saving your collection, and community sharing. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Subscriptions and payments

ShareChef Plus is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple. Payment is charged to your Apple ID, and the subscription renews each month until you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can view, change, or cancel it at any time in your App Store account settings.

ShareChef never sees or stores your payment details. Apple handles the transaction and does not share your card or billing information with us. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether a subscription is active; for that purpose RevenueCat receives your ShareChef account ID and the purchase record Apple issues, and returns only whether your subscription is currently valid. All we store on our side is which plan your account is on, so the app knows whether to apply the free-plan limit. Cancelling your subscription is done with Apple; deleting your ShareChef account does not cancel it.

Micheli Cares (parent check-ins)

Micheli Cares is optional. It lets a teen who is 13 or older connect a parent or guardian, so the parent gets a short check-in on their iPhone or iPad when the teen cooks with Micheli. It is off until both people set it up.

To connect, the teen types a first name, confirms they are 13 or older, and gets a 6-character code that works for 10 minutes. The parent types that code in ShareChef on their own device and confirms they are the teen's parent or guardian. We then store the link between the two accounts and the first name the teen typed. To deliver check-ins, we also store the notification address Apple gives the parent's device.

A check-in says only what the teen told Micheli: that the stove was turned on, that it was turned off, or that a cook ended without the teen saying the stove was off. Micheli cannot see or sense the kitchen. She is a cooking helper, not adult supervision, and a check-in is not a safety guarantee. We do not keep a history of the check-ins that were sent, and check-ins do not include what was cooked or anything else the teen said.

Either person can end the connection at any time in Preferences, under Micheli Cares, by choosing Remove. Removing it stops check-ins right away.

Notifications

ShareChef asks for permission to send notifications only when a parent turns on Micheli Cares, and uses it only for those check-ins. We do not send marketing notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device's Settings.

Third-party services

ShareChef uses Supabase (accounts and data storage), OpenAI (recipe generation and voice processing), Railway (application hosting), Apple together with RevenueCat (subscription purchases and their validation), and the Apple Push Notification service (delivering Micheli Cares check-ins to a parent's device). Each processes data only as needed to operate the app.

Microphone access

ShareChef requests microphone access solely so you can talk to Micheli. Audio is processed in real time and is not used for any other purpose.

Data deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time inside the app: open Preferences, choose "Delete account", and confirm with your password. Deletion is immediate and irreversible — it removes your account, saved recipes, Micheli's memory of you, cooking sessions, shares, and any Micheli Cares connections, codes, and notification addresses tied to your account. Deleting your account does not cancel an active ShareChef Plus subscription; cancel that separately in your App Store account settings. If you cannot access the app, contact us at taibenor93@gmail.com and we will process your request within 30 days.

Children

ShareChef is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. Teens who are 13 or older may use ShareChef, and may choose to connect a parent or guardian through Micheli Cares as described above.
