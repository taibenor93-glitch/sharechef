ShareChef Privacy Policy

Last updated: [SET AT PUBLISH]

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and login credentials, stored securely with Supabase. Recipes you save and content you share are stored with your account. If you use voice features, your speech is transmitted to OpenAI to generate Micheli's responses; ShareChef does not permanently store your voice recordings.

Usage analytics

We collect usage events about how the app is used — such as when the app is opened, a recipe is generated or saved, or a voice session starts and ends — together with a random installation identifier, a session identifier, your account ID when you are signed in, the app version, and timestamps. These events never contain your ingredients, recipes, messages, or voice content. We use them solely to understand and improve ShareChef; we do not use them for advertising and never share them with data brokers. They are stored with Supabase and processed on Railway, and are automatically deleted after 12 months. Deleting your account immediately removes the analytics connected to your account and its installation identifiers.

How we use your data

Your data is used to provide ShareChef's features — generating recipes, powering voice conversations, saving your collection, and community sharing — and, in the case of usage analytics, to understand and improve the app. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Third-party services

ShareChef uses Supabase (accounts and data storage), OpenAI (recipe generation and voice processing), and Railway (application hosting). Each processes data only as needed to operate the app.

Microphone access

ShareChef requests microphone access solely so you can talk to Micheli. Audio is processed in real time and is not used for any other purpose.

Data deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time inside the app: open Preferences, choose "Delete account", and confirm with your password. Deletion is immediate and irreversible — it removes your account, saved recipes, Micheli's memory of you, cooking sessions, shares, and associated usage analytics. If you cannot access the app, contact us at taibenor93@gmail.com and we will process your request within 30 days.

Children

ShareChef is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page.
